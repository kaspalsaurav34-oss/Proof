How proof works

  1. Choose a mission and describe the work you completed.
  2. Add links, screenshots, or uploaded evidence to support your submission.
  3. Track the status as it moves from Draft to review.
  4. Only reviewers or the user can move a submission into Verified in a live product.

Demo safety note

This prototype demonstrates a workflow only. It never auto-verifies or claims real achievements without an actual human review process.

    About PROOF

    We believe opportunity should be earned through evidence, not just words.

    PROOF was created to help young people around the world turn their curiosity into visible proof of work. We want to make it easier for students to demonstrate what they can build, design, solve, and lead — even before they have a formal resume.

    Our mission is to create a trusted, global space where talent is visible, feedback is useful, and growth is measurable. This prototype is intentionally designed as a zero-budget starting point for that bigger vision.
